## Deviations and limitations

No phase-scope deviation. The P004 title, requirement and acceptance condition are preserved. All 365 phase IDs remain present; P005–P365 remain unchecked. The 30 research annotations make the existing optional policy explicit. P165's wording now says timer/IRQ iterations to avoid confusing repeated functional tests with silicon cycle timing.

The claims lint is deliberately bounded. It does not prove arbitrary English, authenticate source contents, infer every transitive dependency or inspect documents outside its declared repository paths. Human milestone review remains necessary; P014's broader compatibility-language audit remains future work.

No CPU/device model, MMIO behavior, proprietary firmware, guest image or runtime gate was added or certified. The hardware registry, generic smoke guest/runner and P003 probe/checker implementation are unchanged. The headless Linux witness and its required functional timer/interrupt behavior remain future implementation obligations.
